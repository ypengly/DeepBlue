DEEP BLUE: ABYSS EXPLORER
=========================
HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip the folder and double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Works offline.
2. Phone/tablet: host the folder anywhere (or open it in a file manager that supports HTML) and rotate to landscape.

DESKTOP: WASD move | Mouse look | Space up | Ctrl/C down | Shift fast | E interact/collect/scan/sell
         F flashlight | Q harpoon | Tab inventory+journal | M map | Esc pause | 1-5 shop at the boat
TOUCH:   Left stick move | Drag screen to look | Up/Down buttons | Sprint toggle | E, light, harpoon buttons | tap shop items

Progress (money, upgrades, journal) auto-saves in your browser. To start fresh, clear this site's data.
